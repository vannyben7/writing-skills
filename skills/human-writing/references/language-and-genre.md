# Language and genre

Use the source's language unless translation is requested. Mixed-language technical
terms, quotation styles, and citation conventions may be intentional. The same
preservation rules apply to Chinese and English, but their sentence patterns differ.

## Chinese

Work with Chinese topic flow and paragraph logic. Do not manufacture an English
subject for every clause, add subjects that the context already supplies, or remove
normal four-character phrases merely because they sound formal.

Look at the function of phrases such as “随着……不断发展”, “在……背景下”, “赋能”,
“多维度”, “深刻揭示”, “具有重要意义”, “不难看出”, “值得注意的是”, and “综上所述”.
They can be useful terms or transitions. Revise when they conceal the specific
relation, repeat what the reader knows, or give every point the same importance.
Keep an actual historical setting, disciplinary concept, or necessary conclusion.

Preserve distinctions such as “相关/导致”, “未发现/不存在”, “不显著/无效”, “多数/全部”,
“可能/必然”, “超过/等于”, “仅在/通常”, and “至少/约”. A less awkward sentence must not
quietly change what was measured, inferred, or denied. Keep Chinese punctuation
that serves syntax; “——” and “（……）” are not automatic defects.

Example, academic:

> 值得注意的是，在控制企业规模后，培训参与度与员工留任率呈正相关（李，2022）。
> 这一发现并不意味着培训导致留任率提高，因为本研究使用的是横截面数据。

Possible revision:

> 控制企业规模后，培训参与度与员工留任率呈正相关（李，2022）。不过，本研究使用
> 横截面数据，不能据此推断培训会提高留任率。

The control, direction, citation, design, and causal limit survive. The negative
claim remains because it constrains interpretation. Do not flag its contrast merely
because it contains “不……”.

## English

Choose clauses for their logical relation, not a target sentence length. Preserve
grammatical passive constructions when the process or result is the topic, the
actor is unknown, or the field expects them. “Samples were stored at −20 °C” needs
no invented technician and no first-person narrator.

Keep disciplinary uses of words such as significant, robust, mediation, landscape,
or leverage when their technical sense matters. Keep lexical compounds such as
“cross-functional” and “end-to-end”; hyphenation does not follow a universal
before-noun/after-verb rule. Respect the applicable style guide.

Example, course essay:

> The case shows the importance of autonomy. The case also shows the importance of
> coordination. The report gives more evidence about coordination: two departments
> used different deadlines. It does not assess employees' motivation.

Possible revision:

> The case points to both autonomy and coordination, but the report develops the
> coordination problem in more detail: two departments used different deadlines.
> It does not assess employees' motivation.

Unequal emphasis comes from the supplied evidence, without inferring that autonomy
is less important or that the deadlines caused a performance loss.

## Academic papers

Preserve the separation between observation, interpretation, prior literature, and
conjecture. A reader should be able to recover the result and its limits after a
paragraph is reorganized. Keep method detail, operational definitions, null results,
and contrasts that delimit the contribution. Do not turn reported associations into
recommendations or replace exposition with imperatives.

When several results receive identical sentences, focus the development on the
distinction already central to the paper. Do not infer priority from effect size
alone, replace precision with a slogan, or append broad societal significance.

## Course essays

Keep the assignment question, word budget, headings, required theories, case facts,
and source roles intact. Let the argument develop through evidence and evaluation;
avoid making every theorist or paragraph equally weighty for visual symmetry.
Do not supply an unargued thesis, invent criticism of a source, or substitute a
confident personal opinion for analysis. First person follows the assignment and
the author's actual stance.

## Personal articles

Retain specific observations, stated emotions, humor, and deliberate changes of pace.
Develop the detail already there instead of manufacturing an anecdote or a more
dramatic self. A quiet ending can remain quiet, and ambivalence can remain unresolved.

Example:

> 我在里斯本待了五天。坡路走得很累，但我喜欢那些小巷。我想再去一次。

This already has a clear voice and can remain unchanged. A rewrite must not invent
a hotel, a month, a tram ride, cold coffee, sore knees, or mixed feelings about the
whole trip. A style sample may guide phrasing; it cannot donate its biography.

## Cross-language use of samples

Only abstract habits with evidence in both languages belong in a bilingual common
layer. A Chinese tendency to state a qualification before the main claim may be a
tentative rhetorical preference for English; it is not evidence about English clause
length, articles, contractions, punctuation, or preferred vocabulary. Use the
target language's normal grammar until appropriate samples or explicit preferences
establish more. Do not translate a profile's example sentences into new content.
